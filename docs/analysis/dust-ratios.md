# Dust-to-Gas and Dust-to-Metal Ratios

Dust-to-gas (D/G) and dust-to-metal (D/Z) ratios are simple-looking diagnostics that are highly sensitive to aperture and bookkeeping choices.

## Dust-to-gas

For a stated aperture,

\[
D/G = M_\mathrm{dust}/M_\mathrm{gas}.
\]

Use integrated component masses when quoting an integrated ratio. A mean of pixel-by-pixel ratios is generally not the same quantity.

## Dust-to-metal

Always state which metal reservoir appears in the denominator. Gas-phase metals, total metals, and gas+dust metals are not interchangeable definitions. CosmicGrain analyses should preserve this distinction explicitly.

## Comparing with observations

Match the observational aperture and metallicity convention as closely as possible. Record the adopted solar metallicity or abundance scale when converting between mass metallicity and oxygen abundance.
